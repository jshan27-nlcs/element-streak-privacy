Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about Element Streak.

How do I play?

Swap two adjacent elemental tiles to make valid chemical formulas. Complete each level's objectives while planning moves for chain reactions and higher scores.

How do I unlock more levels?

Complete the current level to unlock the next experiment. Element Streak features 60 levels across 10 chemistry-inspired chapters.

What is the Formula Book?

The Formula Book keeps track of compounds you discover as you play. Explore their formulas, properties, and real-world uses.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. You can play Element Streak without a network connection.

Where is my progress saved?

Game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. They are not automatically synced to other devices.

Can I adjust sound and vibration?

Yes. Open Settings inside the game to adjust sound and haptic feedback preferences.

Do I need an account?

No. The game does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Are the reactions real chemistry?

Element Streak is inspired by chemical formulas and compounds. Its animated effects are designed for gameplay, not to simulate real chemical reactions.

Troubleshooting

Try these basic steps if something does not work as expected.

Quick checklist
  1. Close and reopen Element Streak.
  2. Restart your device if the issue continues.
  3. Check for an updated version of the app.
  4. If the issue persists, contact support with the level number and a description of what happened.
Important

Keep your progress safe

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its stored data may permanently remove your saved progress.

We recommend avoiding a reinstall unless you understand this risk.
