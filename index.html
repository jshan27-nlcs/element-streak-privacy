Element Streak

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Element Streak is designed to work offline. Based on the current app implementation, the developer does not collect personal information through the app.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Element Streak (the “App”), a chemistry-themed puzzle game, handles information when you use it.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, or other personal information in order to play the App. We do not collect personal information through the App.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores game settings and gameplay progress on your device, such as completed levels, unlocked content, achievements within the game, and sound or haptic preferences. This information is used to maintain your progress and preferences. The App does not transmit this data to the developer's servers.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers.

5. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The App is designed to function without an internet connection. The current implementation does not require an online account, cloud service, or external content download to play.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because it does not request or collect personal information through the App, there is no in-app personal information to disclose to third parties.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings remain on your device. You can remove this locally stored information by deleting the App. Copies included in device backups may remain subject to the device operating system's backup and restoration settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
